---
title: "Generate AI-tailored resumes for any job description"
description: "CareerOps analyzes your profile against a job description, shows your match score and missing keywords, then rewrites your resume with targeted language."
---

The **Generate Resume** page lets you point CareerOps at any job description, run an AI-powered gap analysis against your profile, and produce a tailored resume — all without leaving the app. The workflow runs through four specialized AI agents and ends with a re-scored version of your resume so you can see exactly how much your match improved.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Provide the job description">
    Paste the job URL into the **Job URL** field and click **Fetch** to let CareerOps scrape the page automatically. If the site blocks scraping, paste the full description directly into the text area below the URL field.

    <Tip>
      Fetch works best with public job boards (LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday). For gated postings, copy and paste the full description text instead.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose an AI model">
    Select a provider from the **Select AI Model** dropdown. CareerOps supports five providers:

    | Provider | Default model |
    |----------|--------------|
    | Gemini | `gemini-1.5-flash` |
    | Grok | `llama3-groq-70b-8192-tool-use-preview` |
    | Qwen | `qwen-plus` |
    | OpenAI | `gpt-4o` |
    | Ollama | `llama3` (local) |

    Leave the **Model Name** field blank to use the default. To use a specific model version (e.g., `gpt-4o-mini`), type it in the optional field.

    Enter your API key in the provider's configuration expander. CareerOps saves the key locally in `user_keys.json` so you only need to enter it once. For Ollama, enter your local base URL (default: `http://localhost:11434`) instead of an API key.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Analyze the job description">
    Click **Analyze job description**. CareerOps runs two things in sequence:

    1. A fast keyword and scoring pass that produces your **Match Score** and highlights matching and missing keywords.
    2. The initial three-agent pipeline (Extractor, Researcher, Matcher) that gathers deeper intelligence about the role.

    When the analysis completes, the **Keyword Analysis** section shows green-bordered pills for keywords you already have and grey pills for keywords you are missing. The **Match Score** bar shows your current fit as a percentage.

    The **Quick Requirements** checklist summarizes role-specific fields such as years of experience, visa sponsorship availability, OPT/CPT acceptance, and required certifications.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the Matcher agent's suggestions">
    After analysis, the **AI Agent Analysis (Matcher)** panel displays the Matcher agent's plain-language critique of your resume. This tells you specifically where your current resume falls short relative to the job description and what to emphasize or reword.

    Read through the notes before upgrading — the Writer agent uses them directly when generating your tailored resume.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upgrade your resume">
    Click **✨ Upgrade Resume**. The Writer agent rewrites your resume in Markdown, incorporating the Matcher's feedback and the Researcher's intelligence about the company's hiring process. The new version is saved automatically.

    Immediately after writing, the **Rematcher agent** re-scores the new resume against the same job description. The **Upgraded Analysis (Rematcher)** panel on the right shows:

    - A refreshed keyword breakdown
    - Your new match score with the point improvement displayed (e.g., `+12 pts`)
    - An updated one-sentence recommendation

    If you want to push the score further, click **🔄 Rewrite with more upgrades** to run the Writer and Rematcher again on the already-upgraded version.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Export the resume">
    Click **🖨️ Print View** to open the export dialog. The dialog renders a clean preview of your current resume and offers two download formats:

    - **PDF** — formatted for email attachments and ATS uploads
    - **DOCX** — editable Word document for further manual adjustments

    The filename is generated automatically from the company name, role, and version label (e.g., `CareerOps_Acme_Software_Engineer_Acme_-_Software_Engineer_v1.pdf`).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log the application to your Kanban board">
    Enter the **Company name** and **Role title** in the fields below the resume editor, then click **Save and log application**. CareerOps creates an application card in the **Saved** column of your Kanban board, tagged with the current resume version and match score.

    <Note>
      The Extractor agent auto-fills the Company and Role fields when it can identify them from the job description. Review the values before saving.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Version history

Every time you click **Upgrade Resume**, CareerOps creates a new named version in the format `Company - Role v1`, `Company - Role v2`, and so on. Use the **Version History** dropdown above the resume editor to switch between versions and compare them side by side.

The base version is always called **Master Profile** and reflects your current candidate profile data. Click **🔄 Sync from Profile** to reset it to the latest profile if you have updated your profile since loading the page.

<Note>
  CareerOps uses four AI agents to power the Generate Resume workflow:

  - **Extractor (Agent 1)** — Pulls the company name and job title from the raw description.
  - **Researcher (Agent 2)** — Searches the web for interview process insights and company culture signals specific to the role.
  - **Matcher (Agent 3)** — Compares your resume against the job description and identifies gaps.
  - **Writer (Agent 4)** — Rewrites your resume in Markdown using the Matcher's feedback and Researcher's notes.

  A fifth agent, the **Rematcher**, re-runs the ATS scoring pass after each upgrade so you can see the exact score improvement.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The Writer agent only rewrites the wording and emphasis of your existing experience. It does not fabricate skills, titles, or achievements you do not have. If a required skill is genuinely missing, add it to your candidate profile before upgrading.
</Warning>
