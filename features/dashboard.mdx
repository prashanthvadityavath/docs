---
title: "Monitor your entire job search from the Dashboard"
description: "The CareerOps Dashboard shows your application count, interview and offer tallies, conversion rate, a live pipeline view, and a recent activity feed."
---

The Dashboard is your home base in CareerOps. Every time you open it, you see exactly where you stand: how many roles you've pursued, how many interviews you've landed, and how your pipeline is moving. The data updates in real time as you log applications and move cards through your Kanban board.

## KPI cards

Four metric cards sit at the top of the dashboard. Each card shows a current count alongside a 7-day sparkline so you can spot momentum at a glance.

<CardGroup cols={4}>
  <Card title="Total Applications" icon="briefcase">
    The all-time count of every application you have logged, regardless of stage. Use this as your baseline volume metric.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Interviews" icon="calendar">
    The number of applications currently in the **Interviewing** stage. Watch this number climb as you move cards forward.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Offers" icon="badge-check">
    Applications that have reached the **Offered** or **Offer / Rejected** stage. This is your ultimate success counter.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Conversion Rate" icon="chart-line">
    Offers divided by total applications, expressed as a percentage. Use it to measure how effectively your applications convert to outcomes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Each sparkline plots daily counts for the past seven days (Mon–Sun). A rising sparkline means your search is accelerating; a flat line is a cue to send more applications.

## Application pipeline

Below the KPI row, the **Application pipeline** section renders a compact Kanban view grouped into the same four stages used on the full Applications page: **Saved**, **Applied**, **Interviewing**, and **Offer / Rejected**. Each column shows a count badge and lists every application card belonging to that stage.

Each card displays:
- Company name and role title
- Resume version tag used for that application
- Match score (color-coded: green ≥ 80%, amber ≥ 60%, grey below 60%)
- Date the application was logged

You can move a card to a different stage directly from the dashboard using the **Move to** selector on each card.

## Recent activity

The **Recent activity** feed on the right side of the dashboard shows a reverse-chronological timeline of actions — applications saved, status changes, and resume upgrades. Use it to verify that your recent actions were recorded correctly and to pick up where you left off after a break.

<Note>
  The dashboard requires a candidate profile to be selected. If you see "Select a candidate to view the dashboard," open the header selector and choose or create a profile first.
</Note>
