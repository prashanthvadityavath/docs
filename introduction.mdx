CareerOps gives you a single dashboard to manage every stage of your job search — from crafting a tailored resume for a specific role to tracking where each application stands. Instead of juggling spreadsheets, document versions, and separate tools, you work from one place where AI handles the repetitive analysis so you can focus on the opportunities that matter most.

## What CareerOps does

CareerOps connects four core capabilities that typically live in separate tools:

- **AI resume tailoring** — Paste a job description and CareerOps rewrites your master resume to surface the skills and experience most relevant to that role. The AI pipeline runs through multiple expert personas (Extractor, Matcher, Researcher, and Writer) to produce a factually accurate, keyword-optimized result.
- **Match scoring and gap analysis** — Every job description you submit receives a 0–100 match score against your profile. You also get a list of missing keywords and a checklist of stated requirements so you know exactly what to address before applying.
- **Kanban application tracking** — Move applications through five stages — `Saved`, `Applied`, `Interviewing`, `Offered`, and `Rejected` — on a visual board. The board updates in real time and feeds your analytics.
- **Daily goals and analytics** — Set a daily application target, track your pipeline conversion rates with trend charts, and review a chronological activity timeline to see your search at a glance.

<Note>
CareerOps supports multiple AI providers. You can use **Google Gemini**, **OpenAI**, **Grok**, **Qwen**, or a locally running **Ollama** model. Switch between providers at any time from the **Generate Resume** page — no code changes required.
</Note>
