## How CareerOps works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your master profile">
    Build a single master candidate profile with your skills, experience, education, and projects. CareerOps uses this as the source of truth for every tailored resume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste or fetch a job description">
    Enter a job URL or paste a description directly. CareerOps extracts the role details and compares them against your profile automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyze and tailor your resume">
    Run an AI analysis to get a match score, see missing keywords, and generate a tailored resume version in seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log the application and track progress">
    Save the application to your Kanban board and watch it move through stages — from Saved to Interviewing to Offered.
  </Step>
</Steps>
